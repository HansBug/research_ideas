### 2. 基于原文整理后的自然语言描述

The MINDWALKER lower-limb exoskeleton is supervised by a nine-state gait-assistance FSM that coordinates standing, left or right weight shift, half-step initiation or termination, and full swing progression in both the sagittal and frontal planes. Its main user-triggered guards are based on the ground projection of the combined wearer-exoskeleton `CoM`: when the sagittal and lateral coefficients both fall below their thresholds, the controller initiates the appropriate assisted weight-shift state `S2` or `S6`. From there, state completion automatically advances the machine into the corresponding swing state, while pressing `STOP` sends the FSM to the nearest termination state and then through half swings `S3` or `S7` back to `S1 stand`. Each state carries a distinct reference-generation policy, with online interpolation during weight shift and gait-derived hip and knee references during swing so that the exoskeleton realizes a complete assisted walking loop. In addition, the supervisor monitors `XCoM` deviation during mid-swing and, when the averaged deviation exceeds a threshold over the `40%–50%` swing window, it adapts the `HAA` reference online to widen or narrow the next step and recover lateral stability.
