net power output. The ankle has an added parallel spring to

Weight Acceptance (WA) system. This is a simple spring that

late stance extension torque of the knee as the knee ﬂexes,

Testing of the device was divided into two diﬀerent sections,
bench testing and the patient trials. Bench testing of the devi ce

some cases interfering with the gait cycle. Due to this loss o f
