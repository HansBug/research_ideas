Abstract

PLC (Programmable Logic Controller) is  a special control element that has been designed for sequential relay
control, motion control, process control and data management in industrial automation and control systems.
PLCs have been applied as main control device position at building and intelligent houses automations. Three floors elevator prototype control was carried out in this study used by Siemens's SIMATIC S7-200 CPU 224
model PLC. The hardware of elevator includes a DC motor, limit switches used at every floor and inside and
outside of elevator control buttons and warning lights/indicators, and software includes 12 Networks.  The implemented elevator system wa s converted to an course material for the PLC laboratory.

Key Words:  PLC S7-200, Elevator Automation.
