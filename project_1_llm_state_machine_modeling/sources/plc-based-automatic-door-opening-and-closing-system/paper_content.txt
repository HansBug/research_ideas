Abstract  
The PLC (Programmable  Logic  Controller)  Based  Automatic  Door  Opening  & Closing  System  is a modern  solution  designed  
to enhance  convenience,  efficiency,  and safety  in various  environments  such as commercial  buildings,  residential  complexes,  
hospitals,  and industrial  facilities.  This system  utilizes  advanced  PLC technology  to automate  the operation  of doors,  offering  
seamless  functionality and  reliable performance.  
 
The primary objective of this system is to automate the process of opening and closing doors in response to user input or  
environmental conditions. By employing sensors and actuators, the system detects the presence of individuals ap proaching  
the door and initiates the appropriate action, whether it be opening or closing the door. This functionality improves  
accessibility and convenience for users while also optimizing energy efficiency by minimizing the duration of open -door  
states.  
 
Key components of the PLC Based Automatic Door Opening & Closing System include sensors for detecting motion,  
presence,  and obstacles,  actuators  for controlling  the movement  of doors,  and a PLC unit responsible  for processing  inputs,  
executing  control  logic,  and coordinating  the operation  of the system.  The PLC unit is programmed  with specific  algorithms  
to ensure  smooth  and accurate  door  operation  while  also incorporating  safety  features  to prevent accidents  or collisions.  
 
Moreover,  the system  offers  flexibility  in configuration,  allowing  for customization  according  to the specific  requirements  of 
different  environments.  It can be integrated  with existing  building  management  systems,  access  control  systems,  and security  
systems  to create a comprehensive  solution that  enhances overall  functionality and security.  
 
In conclusion, the PLC Based Automatic Door Opening & Closing System represents a significant advancement in door  
automation technology, offering benefits such as improve d accessibility, efficiency, and safety. Its versatility and reliability  
make it an ideal choice for various applications, contributing to enhanced user experience and operational efficiency in  
diverse  settings.

1.1 System  Design  
 
The design of the automatic door needs to be considered for the purpose of safety and simplicity. This design is  applied 
to public places with large traffic flow. It is designed to take care of the practicality of practical use and  improve 
convenience. The  design of automatic door control system based on PLC needs to consider the  practicality and safety in 
real life, and ensure the safety of the passing object under the guarantee of smooth  operation of the automatic door. This 
paper adopts the photoelectric  sensor to collect whether the object passes  the signal  to the PLC,  and controls  the opening  
and closing  of the automatic  door through  the inverter,  the driving  motor and the transmission device in the PLC control 
actuator; meanwhile, the infrared sensor is opened after  the automatic door is opened. Let the relevant implementing

2. Literature  Review  
 
PLC-based  door opening and  closing  systems  have  gained  significant  attention  in various  industrial  and commercial 
applications due to their efficiency, reliability, and automation capabilities. This literature review  explores  the existing  
knowledge and research  on this  technology.  
 
The development of PLC technology marked a significant shift in the control and automation of various  processes, 
including door operations. Early PLCs were used for simple tasks, but over time, they evolved into  versatile  controllers 
for various applicatio ns. 
 
PLCs are widely employed in door control systems due to their ability to execute complex logic, integrate with  various 
sensors, and provide real -time control. These controllers have proven to be a fundamental component in  achieving  precise  
and efficient  door automation.  
 
2.1 Advantages  of PLC -Based  Systems  
 
Numerous  studies  highlight  the benefits  of PLC-based  door control  systems.  These  advantages  include  improved  safety,  
energy  efficiency,  reduced  human  intervention,  and the ability  to integrate  with other  building  management systems”  [1] 
 
2.2 Applications  of PLC  Door  Systems  
 
Research  demonstrates  the broad  range  of applications  for PLC- based  door systems.  These  systems  are used in 
manufacturing plants for material handling, in warehouses for improved logistics, and in commercial spaces for  
enhancing  security  and accessibility”  [2]

3. roblem  Statement.  
 
The current  manual  door access  systems  utilized  in various  environments  such as commercial  buildings,  educational  
institutions, and residential complexes often suffer from inefficiencies, security vulnerabilities, and lac k of user  
convenience. Traditional systems reliant on physical keys or swipe cards are susceptible to unauthorized access,  
cumbersome  administration  of credentials,  and limited  scalability.  Moreover,  the absence  of automated  mechanisms  results  in 
reduced  operational efficiency  and increased  reliance  on human  intervention  for access  control.  
To address  these  challenges,  there  is a need  for an advanced  automatic  door system  integrated  with RFID  authentication 
technology. This system should leverage the capab ilities of Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs)  and Arduino  
microcontrollers  to automate  door operations  while  ensuring  secure  and convenient  access  for authorized  users.  
 
The primary  problems  to be  addressed  by this project  include:

3.1 Security  Vulnerabilities:  
 
Current manual door access systems may be susceptible to unauthorized entry due to lost or stolen keys/cards,  unauthorized  
duplication, or compromised access codes.  
 
3.2 Operational  Inefficiencies:  
 
Manual door operation necessitates physical  effort and time for users to open/close doors, leading to delays and  congestion  
during peak usage periods.  
 
3.3 Lack  of Scalability  and Flexibility:  
 
Existing door access systems may lack scalability to accommodate growing user populations or evolving access  control  
requirements.  
 
3.4 User  Convenience  and Accessibility:  
 
Manual entry systems may pose challenges for users with mobility impairments or special needs, hindering their  ability  to 
independently  access controlled  areas.  
 
Addressing these issues through the development and implementation of a PLC -based automatic door system  integrated  with 
Arduino -based  RFID  authentication  technology  will enhance  security,  efficiency,  and user experience in accessing 
controlled environments . This project seeks to provide a comprehensive solution that  mitigates security risks, streamlines 
access control processes, and improves accessibility for users while offering  scalability  and flexibility  to adapt  to evolving 
requirements.

5. Conclusion  
 
The PLC -based automatic door opening and closing system lays a solid foundation for future advancements and  
enhancements. One promising avenue for further development is the integration of advanced sensor technologies to  enhance 
the system's capabilities. For instance, the implementation of advanced computer vision algorithms could  enable  the system  to 
detect  and classify  objects  more  accurately,  thereby  improving  obstacle  detection  and avoidance.  
 
Moreover,  exploring  the potential  for cloud  connectivity  could revolutionize  the system's  functionality.  By enabling  remote 
monitoring and control via the internet, users could manage door operations from anywhere, enhancing  convenience and 
accessibility. Additionally, leveraging data analytics techniques could provide valuable insights  into usage  patterns  and 
system performance,  facilitating predictive  maintenance  and optimization.  
 
Furthermore, there is scope for expanding t he application of the system to various settings, such as healthcare  facilities, 
commercial buildings, and public spaces. Tailoring the system to meet the specific needs of different  environments  could  
unlock  new opportunities for innovation and  impact.  
 
Additionally,  integrating  sustainability  features,  such as energy -efficient  components  and renewable  energy  sources,  could  
align  the system with  emerging  environmental  standards  and contribute  to long-term sustainability  goals.  
 
Overall,  the future  scope  of the project  is vast and multifaceted,  offering  numerous  possibilities  for further  research,  
development,  and real-world deployment.
