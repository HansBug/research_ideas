### 2. 基于原文整理后的自然语言描述

The surveillance UAV is controlled by two parallel finite-state machines: one governs camera-view attitude and altitude adjustment, while the other governs relative-distance regulation to the target, and together they emit discrete maneuver waypoints. Starting from `Initialization`, the UAV takes off to a predefined altitude and enters `Sway and Search`, where it performs a 360-degree search motion until the target is detected. Once the target is locked, the controller enters `Track and Hover` if the target remains static, but it can simultaneously activate `Track and Sway`, `Track and Climb or Descend`, and `Track and Forward or Backward` according to horizontal motion, vertical offset, and the target depth relative to the safe-surveillance interval `[Rsafe, Rsur]`. Because the maneuver logic is state based rather than monolithic, the UAV can compose yaw, altitude, and distance reactions in parallel while staying within the dynamic limits `Vqmax`, `Vzmax`, and `Vxmax`. If the target is lost for too many frames, the controller moves into `Lost and Await`; if the target does not re-enter the field of view before the waiting threshold expires, it transitions to `Land` and returns to its home position for touchdown.
