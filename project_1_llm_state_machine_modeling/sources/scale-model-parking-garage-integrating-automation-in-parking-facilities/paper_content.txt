ABSTRACT  
Scale model parking garage gives an idea of modern mechanical fundamentals in an educational and 
research -oriented environment. This thesis aims to develop an automation system of a scale model circular 
parking garage.  The scale model parking garage presents a visual solution to reduce the growing 
challenges for parking with increasing safety, improving operational efficiency, and enhancing space 
utilization.  
To achieve these aims, the study begins by establishing  the fundamental theory  of mechanical system , 
such as ball screw, stepper and servo motors. In addition, it  presents  the concept of program mable logic 
controller, along with motion control technologies  and human machine interface . Based on these 
foundations,  appropriate mechanical  and electrical  component  are selected , such as  AS2021 -OD21 servo 
motor, AM8113 -0F20 -1002  stepper motor and the Beckhoff CX -series  PLC due to its motion control 
libraries, Ethe rCAT communication capabilities, and strong real -time performance. Finally, the thesis 
examines  assembling and testing the project.  
The results of this work demonstrate the successful  designs  of the system architecture, development of 
the control logic  and implementation of human -machine interface. The scale model parking garage 
provides a prototype platform  for preparing a foundation for future testing, implementation, expansion and 
read up on automated parking technologies. Overall, the thesis presents a practical application of 
automation -based system along with modern parking infrastructure.  
 
Keywords  Scale model  parking garage , PLC, I/O systems, servo and stepper motor , ball screw  system

 1 Introduction  
The growing  number of vehicles in cities has created formidable problems  for parking  
framework . Traditional parking garages need  large areas for circulation track.  Due to this 
drawback,  it reduces the efficiency of land use in densely populated metropolitan  
circumstances . City areas  become  more  expensive  and limited . So,  there is a n increasing  
demand for creative  parking solutions , which  can maximize parking capacity while 
maintaining  safety  and operational efficiency . 
An automated parking system  appears  as an effective solution to these problems . Unlike old 
parking garages  where drivers manually park their vehicles , an automated parking systems 
use mechanical equipment as well as control technologies  to transport vehicles to their 
available parking spaces. An automated parking garage can positively  increase the parking 
spaces within a smaller track by terminating  internal driving paths and ramps . It also reduc es 
the human error  risk and improv es safety during vehicle handling.  
One example of a well-organized  automated parking system  is the scale model  parking 
garage. In this layout , vehicles are transported using  lifting mechanisms and rotating 
platforms . Finally , transported vehicles are arranged radially around a central axis. This 
design enab les more efficient use of space compared to the old  parking garages. This type 
of advance parking system is particularly suitable  for the city  areas where land availability is 
limited.  
The implementation of such  a scale model  circular  parking garage  requires  the integration of 
mechanical components with advance d automation technologies . Components like  servo 
motors  and ball screw mechanisms provide controlled motion for lifting . Additionally , stepper 
motors  for positioning and rotating the parking platform. A Programmable Logic Controller 
(PLC) organize s these mechanical movements . It also ensures safe and reliable operation 
of the system. In this thesis, the scale model circular parking garage is developed with  a 
Beckhoff CX -series PLC . The parking system logic programs  are arranged by  TwinCAT 
automation software. Overall, t he scale model circular parking garage  illustrates  PLC-based 
automation and electromechanical system integration to develop a reliable and efficient 
automated  circular  parking garage  for educational  and research purposes.

2.2 Mechanical system   
The scale model parking garage , mechanical systems , like servo motors, stepper motors, 
and ball screws , serve a crucial function in positioning and moving vehicles. The integration

operation during vehicle movement . In the  scale model parking garage , these core

2.2.1  Ball screw systems    
In scale model parking garage , ball screw systems are deployed  to support  in lifting the 
vehicles with high precision and efficiency. These systems convert rotary motion from the 
servo  motor into linear motion . It enabl es smooth and controlled movement of elevators.  Ball 
screw  system  serves  several benefits  in parking systems . The system  offers  higher precision , 
greater load -bearing capacity , and reduced backlash . The motion of ball screw mechanism  
minimizes friction , improves  energy efficiency , and reduc es wear . This results in quieter and 
smoother  operation . (Asy et al., 2020)  
The parking mechanism ensures reliable and consistent motion  by using ball screw systems.  
It also reduc es the risk of jerky movements , ensuring  vehicle safety. In addition , these 
systems requiring less power and distribute force efficiently from the motor while maintaining 
high levels of control over positioning and speed . (Ball Screw Linear Stepper Motor 
Manufacturer - Jkongmotor , n.d.)  
2.2.2  Stepper motors  
Stepper motors are ideal for incremental and accurate  movement applications . In a scale 
model  parking garage , stepper motors are used to control the horizontal movement between  
the parking spaces . Stepper motors are move d in fixed steps or increments  and operated  by 
the pulse from the control system . It ensures smooth position accuracy  that is require d for 
parking system  application . Stepper motors do not require a feedback system for positioning 
because these motors can maintain their position based on the pulse count . Stepper motors

Servo motors are equipped with feedback mechanisms and offer precise control that allow s

2.3 Programmable logic controller (PLC)  
A Programmable Logic Controller (PLC) is a digital computing system . It is  globally used in 
industrial automation to control processes and machines. PLC provides  as the main  control 
unit. In the scale model parking garage , PLC  coordinat es all automated functions like lifting , 
positioning and rotating  vehicles. Its real-time control capabilities , reliability and flexible 
programming create  the PLC essential for ensuring precise movement , smooth operations , 
and safety protocols . The PLC connect s with mechanical components and sensors to 
observe the  system status continuously . It facilitates error -free and efficient vehicle handling  
within the parking place  and execute  instant adjustments.  
An industrial computer, PLC is fundamentally  invented  for controlling field devices and 
reading data in automated processes  system. PLCs vary in size , dependent on  the number 
of input and output (I/O) ports . Larger networked PLCs can support extended racks with 
hundreds of I/O cards to control  more complex operations . On the other hand,  smaller PLCs 
might have a few I/O cards. PLCs are built for harsh industrial environments . PLCs have 
irregular  designs that allow them to go through  extreme conditions  like dust, moisture, 
temperature fluctuations, and other challenging factors. PLCs become crucial  in high -
demand settings such as  automated scale model parking garages through their advanced 
control capabilities  and flexibility .

5 Software implementation  
5.1 PLC project structure and task configuration   
In the scale model circular parking garage system, the Beckhoff  TwinCAT 3 software is used 
to develop logic  sequences . The PLC program is organized in a simple and modular structure  
to make the system easier to understand . 
Figure 12 shows , the main Program Organization Units (POUs) structure  for the project. Each 
POU has a specific purpose in the control system :  
• MAIN (PRG):   
Runs the overall system logic and coordinates communication between all program 
blocks.   


--- Page 23 ---
18 (33) 
 • AUTO_MODE (PRG):   
Controls the automatic parking and retrieval sequence.   
• MAN_MODE (PRG):   
Allows manual operation for  troubleshooting,  testing,  or calibration.   
• big_stepper  (FB) and small_stepper  (FB):   
Function blocks that control the stepper motors, including 
direction  and speed  monitoring.   
• servo (FB):   
Controls the servo  motor and motion  commands  and handles encoder feedback.   
• All_motor_blocks  (PRG):   
Groups and manages all motor -related function blocks so the system can run them 
together smoothly.   
The PLC program uses a cyclic task that runs the main logic at a fixed interval to ensure 
reliable and smooth operation. This task handles important actions such as checking safety 
signals and sending motor commands.  
Less critical functions, such as status updates, run in separate tasks so they do not interrupt

5.4 Servo  and stepper  motor software configuration

 7 Safety system  implementation  
Safety is a vital characteristic  of scale model  parking garage systems during operating of 
electrical equipment  as well as the movement  of mechanical components. The safety system 
implementation ensures reliable operation, prevents potential hazards to users  and 
equipment and protects mechanical components. In this project, safety functions are 
implemented through a combination of PLC-based software logic and hardware devices . 
7.1 Emergency stop  
A normally closed emergency stop push button connected to PLC input as an emergency 
stop function. When the emergency stop button is pressed, it immediately deactivates all 
drives of the motors and halts system operation.  
the PLC program confirms that the motion commands are overridden due to the emergency 
stop signal indication. The system into a safe state and all motors enable signals are 
deactivated. The emergency stop status also provides visual feedback to the operato r 
through the HMI.  
This implementation ensures quick system shutdown in emergency situations to reduce the 
risk of mechanical damage or injury .  
7.2 Motor fault detection  
Motor  fault detection is implemented to observe the operational status of both servo and 
stepper motor systems. Fault signals from motor drives such as overload, communication 
errors, or overcurrent are communicated to PLC input channels.  
The PLC continuously checks the fault signals during system operation. If any fault detects  
during operation the system disables further motion commands and immediately stops the 
affected motor. A fault indication is displayed on the HMI to inform the operator of the specific 
issue.  
This fault detection mechanism prevents damages to mechanical components and motors. 
It also ensures safe system behaviour under fault conditions .

 8 Testing and validation  
System  testing and validation are required to recognize the implemented scale model circular 
parking garage system behaviour under faulty and normal conditions. This phase validates 
the safety of the mechanical and electrical components as well as the electrical  functional 
performance.  
All testing activities are executed in a controlled environment to decrease the risk to 
equipment and operators. The results obtained from testing are used to assessment system 
behaviour and verify compliance with the design objectives .  
8.1 Testing methodology  
A structured and step by step testing methodology is implemented to validate system 
performance. Testing process is initiated with individual component level checking and 
gradually continuation with full system operation. This method support to prevent damag es 
both mechanical and electrical components along with isolate faults and reduce 
troubleshooting time .  
The testing  methodology  includes  the following steps:  
• Component -level testing begins with the verification of individual system components, 
including motors as well as PLC input and output signals, to ensure their proper 
functionality . 
• Subsystem testing then focuses on evaluating integrated sections of the system, 
particularly the motion control functions and the operation of HMI, to confirm correct 
interaction between components.  
• Full system testing follows by validating the complete parking and retrieval 
sequences, ensuring that the entire system operates as intended under normal 
conditions.  
• Finally, safety and fault -condition testing are conducted to verify the system’s 
response to abnormal situations, including the proper functioning of the emergency 
stop and the appropriate handling of fault conditions .
