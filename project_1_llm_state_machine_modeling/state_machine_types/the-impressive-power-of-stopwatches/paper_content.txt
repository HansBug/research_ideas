Abstract. In this paper we deﬁne and study the class of stopwatch au-
tomatawhich are timed automata augmented with stopwatches andun-
observable behaviour . In particular, we investigate the expressive power
of this class of automata, and show as a main result that any ﬁnite or
inﬁnite timed language accepted by a linear hybrid automaton is also ac-
ceptable by a stopwatch automaton. The consequences of this result aretwo-fold: ﬁrstly, it shows that the seemingly minor upgrade from timed
automata to stopwatch automata immediately yields the full expressive
power oflinear hybridautomata. Secondly,reachability analysis of linearhybrid automata may eﬀectively be reduced to reachability analysis of
stopwatch automata. This, in turn, may be carried out using an easy
(over-approximating) extension of the eﬃcient reachability analysis fortimed automata to stopwatch automata. We report on preliminary ex-
periments on analyzing translations of linear hybrid automata using a
stopwatch-extension of the real-time veriﬁcation tool UPPAAL.
