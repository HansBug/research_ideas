ABSTRACT 
Many of the important concepts and results of conventional finite automata theory 
are developed for a generalization in which finite algebras take the place of finite 
automata. The standard closure theorems are proved for the class of sets "recogniz- 
able" by finite algebras, and a generalization of Kleene's regularity theory is pre- 
sented. The theorems of the generalized theory are then applied to obtain a positive 
solution to a decision problem of second-order logic.
