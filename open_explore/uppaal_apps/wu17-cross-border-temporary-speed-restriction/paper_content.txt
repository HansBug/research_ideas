Abstract
Temporary speed restriction server (TSRS) is an important part of the high speed railway train
control system, TSRS not only needs to check the temporary speed restriction orders issued by CTC,but also needs to exchange information frequently with temporary speed restriction server of adjacentdispatching station, on this account TSRS has higher requirements for security and real time property.In order to satisfy the real-time requirements, timed automata sub-models of each equipment areestablished for the working process of cross-border temporary speed restriction, and the overall timedautomata network modelsare built through pa rallel composition of the sub-models. The BNF
(backus-naur form) syntax is used to describe the properties of the cross-border temporary speedrestriction server, and the verification tool of UPPAAL is used to simulate the models. The verificationresults confirm the safety and bounded liveness properties of the system in the interaction process.
Key Words :Cross-Border Temporary Speed Restriction, Timed Automata, UPPAAL, Real-time
1. Introduction
With the rapid development of Chinese high-speed
railway, the requirements of the security and real-timein high speed railway train control system are becominghigher and higher. The temporary speed restriction ser -
ver is an important part of the high speed railway train
control system. Its working process has strict time con -
straints and security features and it’s also a typical real-
time system [1,2]. Therefore, according to the actualoperation of high-speed trains, the temporary speed re -
striction may be setted up in the CTCS (Chinese Train
Control System) grade conversion zone, at this time, theone side of the CTCS grade conversion zone is the pas -
senger dedicated line (CTCS-2/CTCS-3 level controlarea), the other side is the existing speed control area [3 /c45
5]. Because of the complexity of information interactionbetween the devices, the formal methods have been usedto verify the effectiveness of the system design defectsand improve the security of the system.
The formal method is based on the discrete mathe -
matics, and uses strict formal semantics to describe the
characteristics of the system. Timed automata is one offormal methods, it can be used not only to describe thereal value of the clock constraints system, but also to de -
scribe the state of the finite control variables of the sys -
tem. Therefore, according to the CTCS grade conversion
zone, the cross-border temporary speed restriction andthe related equipments of information exchange processto establish the timed automata network models. 2. Analysis of Temporary Speed Restriction in
CTCS Grade Conversion Zone
In the high speed railway control system, the tem -
porary speed restriction server not only carries on the
verification and the storage to the temporary speed re -
striction order, but also must interact with other related
signal equipments. Such as, CTC (centralized trafficcontrol) is responsible to formulate, execute, and cancelthe order. TSRS (temporary speed restriction server) com -
pletes calibration, distribution and synthesis, and feeds
back to CTC. TCC (train control center), RBC (radioblock center), GSM-R (global system for mobile com -
munication-railway) send the TSR to vehicle equipmentimplementation. When the one side of the grade c onver-sion zone is the passenger dedicated line (CTCS-2/
CTCS-3 level control area), the other side is the existingspeed control area. TSRS not only needs to check thetemporary speed restriction orders issued by CTC, butalso needs to exchange information frequently with tem -
porary speed restriction server of adjacent dispatching
station [6,7]. The scene in the CTCS level conversionzone is shown in Figure 1.
The formulation of temporary speed restriction order
follows the principle of “taking the forward line as thespeed limit point”, speed restriction-2 on both sides ofthe TSRS data coverage. Therefore, TSRS-A and TCC-B have to achieve the synchronization of temporaryspeed restriction data interaction, so the temporary speedrestriction orders are generated officially. Alur and Dill in 1994, timed automata is a kind of for-mal method for modeling, which uses clock variables torepresent the time and uses the associated time con-straints to represent the state transition diagrams. 5. Conclusions
This paper applies the formal method of timed auto -
mata for modeling, and establishes the network of timed
automata models in the whole information interactivesystem, meanwhile the validation of the models is car -
ried out by using UPPAAL. The safety and bounded
liveness of the cross-border temporary speed restrictionin the CTCS grade transition zone are verified success-fully, and that ensures the smooth running of the systemwith providing an important basis for the further devel-opment of the system.
