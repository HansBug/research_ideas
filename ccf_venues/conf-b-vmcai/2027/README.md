## 4. Tracks / 口径边界

| Track / 类别 | 官方链接 | 是否关注 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 主会 / research track（单轨） | [VMCAI 2027 Papers](https://conf.researchr.org/track/VMCAI-2027/VMCAI-2027-papers) | 🟡 重点关注 | VMCAI 2027 为单轨会议，track 页与 home 页同内容。主轨含三类投稿，见下表。 |
| Regular papers | 同上 | 🟡 重点关注 | 逐字 `20 pages`（`*Page limits exclude references.`）；**唯一适用双盲**：`Regular paper submissions will undergo a double-blind review process.` |
| Tool papers | 同上 | 🟡 辅助关注 | 逐字 `12 pages`；官方未声明匿名要求；`Authors of tool papers are strongly encouraged to make their tools publicly available and submit an artifact.` |
| Case studies | 同上 | 🟡 辅助关注 | 逐字 `20 pages`；官方未声明匿名要求。 |

投稿共性约束（官方逐字）：`Submissions must follow the Springer LNCS format.`；`All accepted papers will be published in Springer's Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series.`；附录 `not subject to the page limit, but reviewers are not required to read them`；`Simultaneous submissions to other conferences with proceedings, or submissions of material that has already been published elsewhere, are not permitted.`；`For each accepted paper, at least one author is required to register for the conference and present the paper in person.`

Artifact 政策（官方逐字）：`VMCAI 2027 offers authors the option to submit an artifact alongside their paper.`；`Artifact evaluation is conducted alongside the paper review, and its outcome may influence the final acceptance decision.` —— 即 **可选，但可能影响最终录用决定**，且无独立 track / 无独立 deadline。

- 年度论文筛选时优先看与 LLM 状态机建模、验证性质 / 场景、模型检查 profile、缺陷修复闭环直接相关的 research / tool papers。

- **时区来源**：`AoE (UTC-12h)` 在 home/track 页侧栏与 `dates` 页**每行的 `title="Timezone: …"` 属性**中均有声明，两者都是有效来源；但**纯文本提取会丢失属性**，核验必须读原始 HTML。（本页初稿曾写「dates 页无时区声明」，系把提取丢失误读为页面缺失，已于 2026-08-07 更正。）
- 官方只给日期未给钟点，全部主轨节点保留 `待补时刻 AoE / UTC-12h`；**不得**臆造 `23:59`。
- Artifact 无独立 deadline 与独立 track，**不得**照抄 VMCAI 2026 的 `Artifact registration 2025-09-17` / `Artifact submission 2025-09-22`。
- 双盲仅适用 regular papers；tool papers 与 case studies 官方未声明匿名要求，**不得**统一写成全轨双盲（与 SANER 2027 的 full double-anonymous 不同）。
- VMCAI 2028 及以后确认无官方信息（`home/VMCAI-2028` HTTP 404，series 页最新即 2027），不得外推。
- researchr 该 venue 使用大写 slug `VMCAI-YYYY`；后续巡检必须同时探测大小写两种形式。
